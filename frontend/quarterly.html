The Quarterly 500

Each calendar quarter is a separate challenge: everyone re-enters at $500, balances are computed from that quarter's settlements only, and the highest quarter-end balance wins. At least 10 settled predictions in the quarter to appear. No prizes — the balance is the trophy. Ruin is never permanent: next quarter is a fresh $500, and lifetime records never reset.
