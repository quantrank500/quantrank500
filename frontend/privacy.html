Privacy

This page is short because there is little to disclose. That is the point.

What we collect

Anonymous, cookieless page statistics: page views, referrers, and country-level location, via Cloudflare Web Analytics and a self-hosted umami instance. No cookies are set, no identifiers are stored, no individual visitor is tracked, and nothing is shared with advertisers. The umami counts are themselves public — anyone can open the dashboard: the platform that publishes its ledger publishes its traffic.

What we never collect

No accounts, no emails, no names, no phone numbers, no payment details. Your ledger identity is a random UUID plus a secret key that exists only in your own browser — the platform cannot connect it to you.

What you publish

Predictions and their settlements are the product: public, permanent, and exportable by anyone under Creative Commons Zero (CC0) — a public-domain dedication with no rights reserved. Post nothing you want private. An optional display name is public by definition.
